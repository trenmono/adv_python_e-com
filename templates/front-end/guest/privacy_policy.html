1. Introduction

At Aura Beauty, we believe that your personal data is as delicate and unique as your skin. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our radiant ecosystem—from our digital storefront to our personalized skincare diagnostic tools.

By using Aura Beauty, you trust us with your information. We are committed to maintaining that trust by prioritizing your privacy in every technological advancement we pursue.
